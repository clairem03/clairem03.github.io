Teams were pulling their own manual snapshots from legacy systems and getting different answers. I talked with stakeholders across 14 business units to turn conflicting needs into one set of requirements, then designed the ingestion pipeline into the data lakehouse with three IT teams.

Personal project, 2026

Flight Delay Prediction Agent

Ask it a plain question about a flight and it pulls live flight, weather, and FAA data in parallel to answer. The language model handles retrieval and explanation, and a separate scoring engine makes the actual delay call, so every prediction can be audited. Regression tests and tracing keep prompt changes in check.
